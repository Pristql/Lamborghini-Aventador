Lamborghini Aventador

The Lamborghini Aventador is a powerful Italian supercar that was first introduced in 2011 as the successor to the Murciélago. It features a massive 6.5-liter V12 engine that produces around 730 to 780 horsepower, allowing it to reach a top speed of about 350 km/h and accelerate from 0 to 100 km/h in just 2.8 seconds. The car has a seven-speed automatic transmission and an all-wheel-drive system, giving it amazing grip and control. Built mostly from lightweight carbon fiber, the Aventador has Lamborghini’s signature scissor doors and a design that looks both aggressive and futuristic. Its price when new ranged from about $400,000 to $600,000 (roughly ₹3 to ₹5 crore in India). The Aventador was produced until 2022, with its final version being the Aventador Ultimae, and it continues to be remembered as one of Lamborghini’s most iconic and thrilling supercars.
